# EMURGO to Partner with Ergo and Build Blockchain-Based Decentralized Financial Solutions

> **Published:** 2020-06-09 | **Author:** Ergo Platform | **Tag:** Uncategorized | **Type:** news
> **Link:** [https://emurgo.io/en/blog/emurgo-to-partner-with-ergo-and-build-blockchain-based-decentralized-financial-solutions](https://emurgo.io/en/blog/emurgo-to-partner-with-ergo-and-build-blockchain-based-decentralized-financial-solutions)
