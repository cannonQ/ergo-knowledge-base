# Rosen Bridge + Bitcoin Runes Is Live

> **Published:** 2025-11-26 | **Author:** Ergo Platform | **Tag:** Announcements | **Type:** news

*Rosen Bridge has officially launched Bitcoin Runes support on mainnet, opening a direct, decentralized path between Bitcoin assets and the broader UTXO DeFi world. With Runes now live, tokens issued on Bitcoin can move across Ergo and onward to connected networks like Cardano through a single, trust-minimized bridge.*
