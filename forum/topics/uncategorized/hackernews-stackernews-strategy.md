## HackerNews

[news.ycombinator.com](https://news.ycombinator.com/) **Hacker News** (sometimes abbreviated as **HN**) is a social news website focusing on computer science and entrepreneurship. It is run by the investment fund and startup incubator [Y Combinator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Y_Combinator). In general, content that can be submitted is defined as “anything that gratifies one’s intellectual curiosity.”

Generally, threads are fairly hostile to crypto but there is some redemption in the comments. [hn.algolia.com/](https://hn.algolia.com/) provides sorting and searching functionality ontop of the original bare UI.

### What should be posted here

* High quality technical content (cryptography, news)
* ‘AskHN’ posts could be a successful route given the right question.

## Stacker.News

[stacker.news](https://stacker.news/) is a HN clone that where you can earn small amounts of BTC for creating or curating content. Rather than collecting “upvotes” that are not redeemable or transferable.

### What should be posted here

* High quality technical content (cryptography, news)
* Anything relating to Bitcoin<>Ergo
