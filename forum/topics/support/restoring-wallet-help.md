### Post #2 — @ZeroKnowledge (2021-04-22)

You can try other ergo wallets. One way would be by downloading and installing an ergo full node, but I don’t really recommend it (takes a long time). Maybe try again restoring in Yoroi on a different computer, or force Yoroi to resync (Yoroi has problems with too many users at the moment which makes their service slow and inaccurate at times). In any case, if you have the CORRECT recovery phrase you should be just fine.
