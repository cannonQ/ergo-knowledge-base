### Post #2 — @BOTNIQ (2021-10-20)

I was able to figure it out. There is a refund transaction button in the transaction history tab on the ErgoDEX site. The transaction history is found by hitting the grey button to the left of your ERG balance in the top right corner on the DEX site.
