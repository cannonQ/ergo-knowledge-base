### Post #3 — @Grayman (2023-02-23)

Choose a wallet from here: [GetErg | Ergo Platform](https://ergoplatform.org/en/get-erg/#Wallets)  
I like the Ergo Wallet App, and SAFEW.

Next, create a wallet, and store your seed phrase in a safe place.  
The wallet address for this new wallet is available from there and should look something like this:  
9hJAqQFJ8qLiDbc8T77Az9L1hlQ3dkrkzzrSi3tvgQAsWuTtvtQ

That is your target for the miner app running on whatever mining hardware you have.

I hope this helps.
